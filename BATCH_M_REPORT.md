## Evidence and ethical limitations

- **14 claim-level annotations** reference descriptions or editions in the Academy of Korean Studies and National Institute of Korean History. Direct means the linked institutional article explicitly supports the factual proposition; contextual means historical context or a document genre, not direct confirmation of every explanatory clause.
- Examples include Academy of Korean Studies articles on `족보`, `분재기`, `별급문기`, `안채`, `의녀`, `의과`, `사례`, `상례`, and the NIKH reference on genre painting. These references were checked as background institutional sources, **not** primary-document transcriptions personally examined page by page in this batch.
- Prescriptive ritual manuals, property and inheritance documents, surviving elite homes, formal offices, and genre paintings are not representative of all ordinary households. The prose treats women's work and the lives of enslaved people as important but does not invent personal testimony from unrecorded lives. This remains editorial synthesis, not a peer-reviewed corpus.
