## VII.6 — Late Joseon agriculture and the economic transformation

1. [National Institute of Korean History, original-source collection, Later Joseon economy](https://contents.history.go.kr/mobile/hm/list.do?periodCode=hm_age_60&typeCode=hm_ty_020). Covers farming and wealth differentials, proposals for land reform, artisans, mining, periodic markets and Qing border trade; collection includes contrasting primary writers and changing dates.
2. [NIHK, formation and expansion of periodic markets](https://contents.history.go.kr/mobile/nh/view.do?levelId=nh_030_0060_0020_0040_0010). Research synthesis on the geographical origins and changing market networks; five-day schedules developed rather than arriving everywhere simultaneously.
3. [NIHK, Joseon commodity and money economy](https://contents.history.go.kr/eh_kk/teach/tong/iv/iv34.html). Teaching synthesis describing gongin suppliers, private merchants, markets, copper coinage and interregional networks. Simplifications and round market counts must be read as teaching-level approximations.
4. [NIHK, agriculture and rural circulation](https://contents.history.go.kr/mobile/km/view.do?levelId=km_027_0050_0050_0030). Context for agricultural production, land records and mixed household livelihoods.
5. [NIHK, rice and cloth as money](https://contents.history.go.kr/mobile/km/view.do?levelId=km_027_0050_0050_0040). Evidence that cloth, grain and coin systems overlapped in time; do not describe the advent of coinage as instantaneous replacement of all other exchange.
6. [Academy of Korean Studies, Daedong Sangjeong Law](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0014255). Regional modification of tribute-tax arrangements and recognition that a uniform land assessment could be unworkable in districts with different agricultural conditions.

**Interpretive boundaries:** Late-Joseon administrative registers are not modern population censuses; recognized taxable acreage is not a complete land survey. The appearance of a cultivation method does not establish adoption on all farms. Policies and treasury regulations show official intentions, not uniform county-level compliance. Credit, tenancy and commercial exchange were heterogeneous rather than a single proto-modern capitalist model. The preserved `VII.6.5` text was expanded **without** rewriting its original metadata to retrofit citations.

## IX.4 — Authoritarian government and democratic opposition, 1953–1991

1. [National Archives of Korea, April Revolution chronology](https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/419/info01.do?menuId=01). Precise chronology of March 15 and April 1960, Kim Ju-yeol, student/professor demonstrations, Rhee's resignation, Second Republic and May 16, 1961.
2. [National Archives, 1960 election and April Revolution investigations](https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/history/samilos/sub2_1.do). Later judicial case records and government documents require separation of contemporaneous charges, decisions and subsequent interpretation.
3. [National Archives, Military Revolutionary Committee, 1961](https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/organ/organBasicInfo.do?code=OG0036770&isProvince=&mapInfo=mapno1). Describes committee, command structure, restrictions on assemblies and press and claimed emergency powers.
4. [National Archives, state newsreel for May 16](https://theme.archives.go.kr/viewer/common/archWebViewer.do?archiveEventId=0047680388). A *regime-produced primary artifact*, not a neutral assessment of its self-described “revolution.”
5. [NIHK, October Yushin, 1972](https://contents.history.go.kr/mobile/kc/view.do?levelId=kc_i500200). Records Park's October 17 emergency, dissolved legislature and establishment of the indirect-selection Yushin system.
6. [Academy of Korean Studies, Gwangju Democratic Movement](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0038496). Scholarly synthesis of May 18–27, 1980, differentiated from derogatory authoritarian-era labels.
7. [National Archives, Gwangju documentary overview](https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/koreaOfRecord/5_18.do). Context for later recognition, investigations and the **dated** casualty count in a post-1987 government accounting. Do not treat one count as the final total for every category of harm.
8. [National Archives, 1987 Ninth Constitutional Amendment](https://theme.archives.go.kr/next/rule/sub2_10.do). Records October 29, 1987 referendum and February 25, 1988 effective date; direct five-year single-term presidency, expanded procedural safeguards and parliamentary powers.

**Interpretive boundaries:** Separate national security conditions from government's use of them to suppress domestic opponents. Electoral victory is not equivalent to fair competition when courts, security agencies and constitutional rules are constrained. The overthrow of a dictator is not the instant consolidation of a democracy. Government newsreels and emergency proclamations document **regime self-justification**. Gwangju victim totals require institution, category and date. The 1987 settlement was consequential but did not erase former state personnel or end later contestation.
